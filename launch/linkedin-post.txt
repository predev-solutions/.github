Every Egyptian developer has written this regex at least once.

Is this a real national ID? Is 010 or 015 a valid mobile prefix? What if the user types the number in Arabic digits, or with +20, or with dashes? Which governorate is code 21?

We got tired of rewriting it in every project, so we open-sourced it.

egypt-validators is a small PHP package, with ready-made Laravel rules, that does it once and properly:

- Validates Egyptian national IDs and reads the birth date, age, governorate and gender from them
- Rejects impossible dates, leap-year mistakes and unknown governorate codes
- Normalizes mobile numbers in any format, including Arabic digits, +20 and 0020
- Tells you the carrier: Vodafone, e&, Orange or WE
- All 27 governorates in Arabic and English
- Laravel rules like EgyptianNationalId::make()->minAge(21)

45 tests, MIT licensed, free to use in commercial projects.

composer require predev-solutions/egypt-validators
